\section{Nonunit finite multiplication constants}
\label{sec:constant-table}
The table gives the constants $c_{N,M}^{P}$ in chronological input
order: first $N$, then $M$. Every unlisted nonunit channel is zero; the
unit channels have coefficient $1$ on their compatible multiplets. The
names and representation degrees are those of the fixed basis in Section~\ref{sec:finite-basis} and the accompanying
exact JSON data.
The code names correspond to the multiplets of
Section~\ref{sec:finite-basis} as follows. The last column gives highest
weight, independently of path degree.
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
\text{name}&\text{mathematical multiplet}&\text{path degree}&\text{highest weight}\\\hline
\texttt{a\_ij}&a_{ij}&1&0\ (T\leftrightarrow A,B),\ 1\ (\text{outer})\\
\texttt{t},\texttt{x\_i},\texttt{y\_i}&t,x_i,y_i&2&0\\
\texttt{b0\_ik},\texttt{b1\_ik},\texttt{b2\_ik}&b^0_{ik},b^1_{ik},b^2_{ik}&2&0,1,2\\
\texttt{za\_ij}&Z_{\rm aux}a_{ij}&3&\text{as }a_{ij}\\
\texttt{s\_i}&s_i&4&0
\end{array}
\]
Here $Z_{\rm aux}$ is the central element with corners
$(t,x_A-3y_A,x_B+y_B,x_C-3y_C,x_D+y_D)$, not the generator
$Z=N(0,0,1)$ of the centre section. The tensor contraction is exactly
the unnormalized transvectant in \eqref{tr:eq:T},
in the input order
$N$ then $M$; thus no additional sign conversion is implicit in this table.
